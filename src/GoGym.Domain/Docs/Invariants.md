# Invariants
## Session Invariants
- A session cannot contain more than the maximum number of participants
- A reservation cannot be cancelled for free less than 24 hours before the session starts

## Gym Invariants
- A gym cannot have more rooms than the subscription allows

## Room Invariants
- A room cannot have more sessions than the subscriptoin allows
- A room cannot have two or more overlapping sessions

## Subscription Invariants
- A subscription cannot have more gyms than the subscription allows

## Trainer Invariants
- A trainer cannot teach two or more overlapping sessions

## Participant Invariants
- A pariticipant cannot reserve overlapping sessions
